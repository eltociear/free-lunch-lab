FAQ

FAQ
Is this a real basic income?

Not the government kind. Here, “UBI” means projects like GoodDollar and BioMatrix that give tokens to every registered person, repeatedly and without conditions. Japan has no national basic income yet.

Do I lose anything by using a referral code?

Usually not. Fees and conditions are the same, and most services give the new user a bonus too. The referrer also gets a reward, which is why this page is marked PR.

Are the rewards taxed?

In Japan, crypto counts as miscellaneous income when you sell or spend it at a profit, and points are usually treated as occasional income. Rules differ by country, so check your local tax authority if the amounts add up.

Can I use these outside Japan?

Services marked “Global” (GoodDollar, ME Pass, BioMatrix, Grass, Honeygain) work outside Japan. The rest are for Japan residents.
